Blendy Play: Powered by CineJoy
How to install on Windows
===========================

You need: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection.
Blendy Play doesn't need installing. You unzip it and run it.


1. UNZIP IT
-----------
- Right-click Blendy-Play-Windows-x64.zip and choose "Extract All...".
- Pick a place to keep it, for example your Documents folder or
  C:\Users\<your name>\Apps, and click "Extract".

  Don't run Blendy Play from inside the zip without extracting it first.
  It won't work properly that way.


2. START IT
-----------
- Open the "Blendy Play" folder you just extracted.
- Double-click "Blendy Play.exe".


3. IF WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"
----------------------------------------------
This appears the first time because Blendy Play is a new app that hasn't
been code-signed. To continue:
- Click "More info".
- Click "Run anyway".

You only need to do this once.


4. MAKE A SHORTCUT (optional)
-----------------------------
- Desktop: right-click "Blendy Play.exe", choose "Show more options"
  (Windows 11), then "Send to" > "Desktop (create shortcut)".
- Taskbar: start Blendy Play, then right-click its icon on the taskbar
  and choose "Pin to taskbar".


UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
-------------------------
Close Blendy Play, delete the old "Blendy Play" folder, and extract the
new zip in the same place. Your sign-in and settings are kept.
(Versions before the rename used a folder called "MK-Flix": delete that
one instead, and any shortcuts to MK-Flix.exe.)


UNINSTALLING
------------
- Delete the "Blendy Play" folder, and any shortcuts you made.
- To also remove your sign-in and settings, press Windows key + R,
  type %APPDATA%\MK-Flix and press Enter, then delete that folder.
  (It keeps the app's former name, MK-Flix.)


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Press F11 or use the fullscreen button on a video to watch fullscreen.
  Press Esc to leave fullscreen.
- Press Alt to show the menu bar.
- Ads and pop-ups are blocked automatically.
- Blendy Play doesn't host any films or shows. It's a window onto cinejoy.pk.


Enjoy! NutFlix just got better ;)
