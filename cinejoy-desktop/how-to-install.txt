MK-Flix: Powered by CineJoy
How to install on Windows
===========================

You need: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection.
MK-Flix doesn't need installing. You unzip it and run it.


1. UNZIP IT
-----------
- Right-click MK-Flix-Windows-x64.zip and choose "Extract All...".
- Pick a place to keep it, for example your Documents folder or
  C:\Users\<your name>\Apps, and click "Extract".

  Don't run MK-Flix from inside the zip without extracting it first.
  It won't work properly that way.


2. START IT
-----------
- Open the "MK-Flix" folder you just extracted.
- Double-click MK-Flix.exe.


3. IF WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"
----------------------------------------------
This appears the first time because MK-Flix is a new app that hasn't
been code-signed. To continue:
- Click "More info".
- Click "Run anyway".

You only need to do this once.


4. MAKE A SHORTCUT (optional)
-----------------------------
- Desktop: right-click MK-Flix.exe, choose "Show more options"
  (Windows 11), then "Send to" > "Desktop (create shortcut)".
- Taskbar: start MK-Flix, then right-click its icon on the taskbar
  and choose "Pin to taskbar".


UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
-------------------------
Close MK-Flix, delete the old "MK-Flix" folder, and extract the new zip
in the same place. Your sign-in and settings are kept.


UNINSTALLING
------------
- Delete the "MK-Flix" folder, and any shortcuts you made.
- To also remove your sign-in and settings, press Windows key + R,
  type %APPDATA%\MK-Flix and press Enter, then delete that folder.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Press F11 or use the fullscreen button on a video to watch fullscreen.
  Press Esc to leave fullscreen.
- Press Alt to show the menu bar.
- Ads and pop-ups are blocked automatically.
- MK-Flix doesn't host any films or shows. It's a window onto cinejoy.pk.


Enjoy! NutFlix just got better ;)
